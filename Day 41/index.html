Kayla's Favorite Movies!

My three favorite movies of all time.

Spirited Away

I adored this movie as a kid. Visually stunning even today with the gouache hand drawn animation and fun setting.

Scooby-Doo (Live Action)

Another movie I loved as a kid. Not exactly kid appropriate though. I watch it every Halloween.

Hereditary

Horror film. Genuinely stunned me the first time I watched it. I like to rewatch it with friends.
